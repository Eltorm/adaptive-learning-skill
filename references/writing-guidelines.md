# Coherent cognitive-flow writing

Use this reference while drafting a chapter or explanation. The internal graph
can be explicit in planning while the reader sees a small, motivated path.

## Paragraph-level flow

Prefer a real question or observable tension to open a section, then move
through `phenomenon -> problem -> limitation -> need -> idea -> mechanism ->
consequence -> next question`. Use a transition only when the underlying
reasoning exists: “因此” for consequence, “但” for a limitation, “于是” for a
response, “这也解释了” for a derived connection, and “如果改动条件” for a
counterfactual. Do not sprinkle connectors as decoration.

Avoid repetitive scaffolding such as “相关概念包括…”, “此外…”, or a
standalone “知识关联” section. A concise explicit anchor is appropriate when a
strong connection would otherwise be missed; send merely interesting links to
a deferred branch.

## Depth without bloat

Embed assumptions, mechanism, alternatives, and boundaries in the causal
story. Use a box/table or short aside only when it prevents a real ambiguity.
Do not force every What/Why/How question to become a heading. End a module with
what the learner can now predict or reconstruct and what remains untested.

## Anti-pattern test

If deleting a paragraph leaves all predictions, decisions, and causal links
unchanged, it is probably decorative exposition. If an analogy cannot be
translated back into the precise mechanism and its limits, remove it.
