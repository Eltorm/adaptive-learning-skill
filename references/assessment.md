# Mastery and repair patterns

Use the smallest assessment that distinguishes recognition from understanding.

| Dimension | Evidence of mastery |
| --- | --- |
| Concept | accurate explanation and distinction from a near-neighbour |
| Mechanism | causal account that predicts an intervention or changed assumption |
| Formal/mathematical | reconstructs a derivation, calculation, or proof sketch |
| Application/implementation | chooses or executes a method in a novel case |
| Limits | diagnoses failure, trade-off, or boundary condition |
| Transfer | maps the principle to a different domain or representation |

Repair loop: identify the failed dimension → trace its lowest missing required
prerequisite → teach a minimal bridge → retest with a new surface form. Give
solutions after an attempt. A fluent summary without prediction or reconstruction
is evidence of familiarity, not mastery.
