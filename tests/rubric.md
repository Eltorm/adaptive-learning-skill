# Evaluation rubric (0–5 each)

Score generated behavior, not whether it copied headings. A 3 means usable MVP;
4 means strong and 5 means unusually robust.

1. **Scale and scope** — proportional breadth and no fixed chapter inflation.
2. **Entry/prerequisites** — starts at the requested cut-in point; tiers and
   tiny diagnostics are actionable.
3. **Depth and mechanism** — target L-level is evidenced by explanation,
   derivation, implementation, limits, or research critique as appropriate.
4. **Knowledge connections** — dependencies and at least one consequential
   cross-link appear naturally, with a visible mainline/foreshadow/side-branch
   choice and without a relationship dump.
5. **Domain adaptation** — domain-appropriate evidence of understanding.
6. **Reliability** — uncertainty/status is calibrated; current/contested claims
   are verified or explicitly limited.
7. **Assessment/adaptation** — reconstruction, prediction, or transfer plus a
   diagnostic next-step rule; unanswered checks remain untested.
8. **Cognitive flow** — phenomenon/problem → need → idea → mechanism →
   consequence remains readable.

Record one concrete failure and one minimal rule change for any score below 3.
